Plain-language policy

Privacy

Last updated August 28, 2026

Your work stays in your browser

Motion Feel Lab has no account system and sends no curve values, exports, or settings to us. Your most recent curve is saved in your browser’s local storage so you can return to it. Share links encode the curve in the URL itself.

What we collect

The app includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, fingerprinting, or third-party tracking scripts. Our hosting provider may process standard request logs such as IP address, user agent, requested path, and time for security and reliability.

Your choices

Use “Reset curve” in the lab to replace saved settings, or clear this site’s storage in your browser to remove them completely. The service worker stores only app files for offline use.

Contact

Questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
